## Semantics B should know
- `dup_text`: chain rule. Within (make, model, year, system, first 200 normalized chars),
  ordered by (ldate, odino), a complaint is flagged when the previous one is ≤ 30 days earlier.
  The first complaint of a burst is never flagged; count `NOT dup_text` to count it once.
- `severe` = crash or fire or injured > 0 or deaths > 0; `described` = redacted text ≥ 20 chars.
- Dates, flags and vehicle fields are per complaint (`complaint_doc`) and copied to its rows.
- ODINOs whose rows name different vehicles are excluded (`odino_vehicle_conflict`).
